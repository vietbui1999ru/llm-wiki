# Embeddings

Dense vector representations of text (or other data) produced by an encoder model. Each token or passage is projected into a high-dimensional space where semantic similarity corresponds to geometric proximity. Core primitive for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), semantic search, and re-ranking pipelines.
